04 / Engineering gate

What still needs evidence

  • FF/SF column continuity: source-matched upper-level sections and elevations, beam-column joints and the architectural/structural datum.
  • Frame support: shear, flexure, anchorage and gravity/earthquake transfers at G2; correct I4 original vs owner working I3.
  • Foundation: CF1 eccentricity, G1 drawn/scheduled footing mismatch, source reactions and soil bearing/punching.
  • Slabs & stairs: genuine closed shell contours, openings, load path and local structural element sections.
  • Seismic and soil: NBC 105:2025 site class and physical parameters, response spectrum, diaphragm justification, accidental torsion and cracked stiffness.
  • Adverse safety: historical F3 NOT SAFE IN ONE WAY SHEAR is still unresolved.
