04CHAPTER 04 · MODELLING & SOLVER

OpenSees model, assumptions and analysis method

We use OpenSees instead of recreating ETABS screenshots; every solved case preserves machine-readable numerical evidence.

Five real static 3D OpenSees solves · Model still hypothetical

4.1 · FE discretization and boundary conditions

OpenSeesPy 3D elasticBeamColumn, six degrees of freedom per node, fixed-base supports, assumed beam topology and source-derived column coordinates. Default trial includes 165 members, 92 nodes and two unverified beam connections repeated at three levels.

Selfweight, distributed wall loads, actual slab shells and complete diaphragm assignments are not represented in the default five-case report.

4.2 · Solving and checking

The solver executes GRAVITY, EQ_PLUS_X, EQ_MINUS_X, EQ_PLUS_Y and EQ_MINUS_Y. Each run checks global force and moment equilibrium.

ΣF(applied) + ΣF(reactions) ≈ 0ΣM(applied) + ΣM(reactions) ≈ 0

Passing equilibrium proves numerical consistency, not that source drawings, actual mass or member capacity are correct.
