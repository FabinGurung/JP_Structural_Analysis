What remains for design-level completion

A verified source-model solver run must produce actual nodal displacements, support reactions, modal eigenpairs, diaphragm actions and member end-force envelopes. Separately, approved reinforcement drawings and real footing schedules are necessary for a construction BBS/BOQ. This catalogue is the native geometry and load-intake foundation on which those audited stages can build; it does not substitute simple-support estimates for real multi-storey frame forces.
