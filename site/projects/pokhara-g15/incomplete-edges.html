What evidence would change the verdict?

For each original source slab: matched ULS/SLS analysis joint and shell mesh element IDs; compatible shell-edge/frame restraints and releases; load direction and genuine supported equilibrium; member-end and foundation support reactions; drawings confirming openings and cantilever anchorage; and an independent engineer’s full strength/serviceability review. Without these, the structural-load field remains unknown, never assumed zero or silently redistributed.
