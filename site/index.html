03 · ENGINE

Small modules, explicit responsibilities.

The repository separates evidence handling, normalized structural meaning, numerical engines, QA, validation and reporting instead of mixing them into one opaque script.

INGEST

Source inventory

SHA-256, classification, source authority and parser lineage.

NORMALIZE

E2K → canonical model

Conservative extraction of structural meaning without silently guessing ambiguity.

SOLVE

OpenSees adapter

Elastic frame seed, explicit eigensolver behavior and reproducible configuration.

BENCHMARK

Transparent native solvers

Moment distribution, direct stiffness, load combinations and focused checks.

QA

Fail-closed gates

Model/reference checks and professional states that preserve unresolved items.

REPORT

SAR artifacts

HTML plus machine-readable result, validation, assumption, conflict and manifest records.
