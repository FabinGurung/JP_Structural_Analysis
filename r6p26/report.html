Executive Summary

This is a runnable calculation report. Its 3D elastic frame reactions, signed end actions and displacement results are generated afresh from source-labelled input data in GitHub Actions, with six-axis force/moment equilibrium and reproducible artifact hashes. Actual column positions come from the pinned Narayani source DXF extracts. Repeated storey columns, nearest-column beam connections, floors and area-proxy loads remain explicitly assumed.

Engineering acceptance is different from numerical execution. The calculation evaluates dead and imposed gravity plus fixed lateral probe loads; it does not calculate NBC 105:2025 design seismic forces, strength capacities, slab/footing punching, foundation soil safety, or ETABS numerical parity.

CHAPTER 01REFERENCE REPORT STRUCTURE

1.0 Introduction

1.1 Executive Summary

This chapter preserves the original report's intent: describe a residential reinforced-concrete building and the structural calculations supporting its assessment. Our numerical results are independently regenerated; the original report remains a source to audit, not a trusted substitute for FE analysis.

1.2 Structural Modelling

Model: 3D, six degrees of freedom per node, linear-elastic elasticBeamColumn members. Distinct geometric column foundations are fixed idealizations for preliminary inspection. Candidate beam endpoints are projected onto the nearest source ground-floor column axis; non-close and duplicate mappings are recorded rather than quietly discarded. No floor shell plate or rigid-diaphragm constraint is yet imposed.

1.3 Structural System of the Building

The source describes a special moment-resisting frame (SMRF). This model approximates rigid beam-column frame joints as a calculation hypothesis. It cannot prove SMRF detailing, ductility, strong-column/weak-beam capacity, joint shear or foundations. Original GF axis I4 and owner-working I3, G2 junction, CF1, and G1 inconsistencies remain open.

CHAPTER 03INDEPENDENT METHOD

3.0 Analysis and Design Procedure

  1. Read eleven CAD-derived registers at immutable commit; verify original structural drawing SHA identity.
  2. Normalize 23 source GF column marks and 21 footing outlines; distinguish proposed Block B.
  3. Preserve original DXF beam candidate handles (32 GF, 17 FF, 17 SF) and audit all assumed endpoint connections.
  4. Extend source GF column lines to three elevated levels under an explicit 3.05 m/storey hypothesis.
  5. Use source section schedules where declared and labelled section defaults for unassigned beam/upper-column details.
  6. Construct physical 3D six-DOF nodes; one elastic frame element per approved calculation hypothesis; omit duplicate/zero mapped spans.
  7. Compute actual section/membership selfweight using 20 kN/m³; avoid slab/beam depth double count.
  8. Apply floor areas as uniform nodal gravity proxies, not pretend one-/two-way shell load transfer.
  9. Independently solve DL, LL, PX10 and PY10 with OpenSeesPy; retain signed member I/J forces.
  10. Read fixed-base support reactions and global six-axis equilibrium including force-position moments.
  11. Generate traceable per-member CSV, JSON casebook and SHA256 receipt with explicitly bounded conclusions.
  12. Block engineering/compliance claims until actual slab, member, joint, soil and seismic source checks are completed.
